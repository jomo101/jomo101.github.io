Simulation engines

Wilson-clover RHMC gauge-field sampler C

A lattice QCD sampler written from scratch in C, no external libraries. Wilson and clover fermions, even/odd preconditioning, and $n$-th-root pseudofermions via the Clark–Kennedy construction: Remez coefficients, multi-shift CG with converged-shift freezing, adaptive spectral windows. Validated against physics identities — reversibility, finite-difference force checks, $\langle e^{-\Delta H}\rangle = 1$ — with an $N_f = 1{+}1$ run reproducing a validated $N_f = 2$ ensemble.

Replacing leapfrog with Omelyan 2MN and a two-level Sexton–Weingarten splitting, at equal solve budget on $6^3\times 8$, $\kappa = 0.13$:

MPI — 1-D $t$-slab decomposition behind one communication seam; identical CG iteration counts and 13–14-digit agreement across 1, 2 and 4 ranks.

Batched rational solver — columns of {pseudofermion} × {shift} with per-column recurrences and tolerances, converged columns freezing out. Bridged into a collaborator's AVX-512 Wilson-clover inverter so one pair of Dslash sweeps serves every column.

PyCALQ Python

Correlator analysis through the Lüscher quantization condition. fvspectrum extracts the finite-volume spectrum; QC2 fits phase shifts and mixing angles against $\det(K^{-1} - B) = 0$ with coupled-channel K-matrix models and bootstrap errors. Absorbs my standalone NN-tools fitter.

17.7 s → 40 ms per $\chi^2$ evaluation, 440×. Hoisting the parameter-independent $B(E)$ out of the optimizer loop onto a Chebyshev–Lobatto grid with the poles divided out, then solving roots vectorized across a process pool. Made multi-wave fits with $N = 1000$ bootstraps tractable.
